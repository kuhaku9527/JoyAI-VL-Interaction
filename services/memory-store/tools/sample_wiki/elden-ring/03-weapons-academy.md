# Weapons and the Academy

## Grafted Blade Greatsword
The Grafted Blade Greatsword is found on the corpse of the Dreaded Corsus near the shore. Where is the Grafted Blade Greatsword? It rests on the giant's back after you win the bout.

## Academy Glintstone Key
Enter the Raya Lucaria Academy with the Academy Glintstone Key. The Academy Glintstone Key opens the sealed gate of the Raya Lucaria Academy so you can reach the debate parlor.

## Meteoric Ore Greatsword
Find the Meteoric Ore Greatsword in the Caelid swamp near the Astray. The Meteoric Ore Greatsword is a gravity greatsword dropped by a falling star that crashed into the swamp.

## Gravity Bolt
The Meteoric Ore Greatsword weapon skill is Gravity Bolt, a gravity projectile that pulls foes toward you. Gravity Bolt is the signature skill of the Meteoric Ore Greatsword.

## Starcaller Cry
The Starscourge Greatsword skill is Starcaller Cry, which pulls enemies into a gravity well and bursts. Starcaller Cry is the weapon skill of the Starscourge Greatsword once owned by Radahn.

## Red Wolf of Radagon
Radagon's Soreseal is dropped by the Red Wolf of Radagon in the Academy lobby. The Red Wolf of Radagon guards the way to the debate parlor and drops Radagon's Soreseal when slain.
