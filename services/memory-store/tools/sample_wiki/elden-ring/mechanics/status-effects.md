# Status Effects

## Scarlet Rot

A deadly buildup that deals damage over time. Malenia and the Lake of Rot are
the main sources. Cure it with a Purifying Purple Lily or by resting at a site
of grace. Flame, Cleanse Me also removes it.

## Bleed

Builds up with successive hits; when triggered it deals a burst of damage
scaled to max HP. The Hand of Malenia and most katanas inflict bleed.

## Frostbite

Reduces enemy resistance temporarily after buildup. Often paired with physical
weapons.

## Poison

Similar to rot but milder; cured by Poison-curing boluses.
