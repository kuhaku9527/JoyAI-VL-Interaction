# Stormveil Castle

Stormveil Castle is the legacy dungeon guarded by Godrick. The intended path
climbs the outside cliff via a hidden spirit spring, but a secret path through
the sewer lets you skip most of the castle.

## Secrets

The sewer entrance is behind the locked door past the first grafted scion.
It bypasses Margit-adjacent combat and drops you near the Godrick fog gate.
The courtyard has a falling chandelier you can shoot to crush a knight.

## Loot

Expect the Grafted Blade Greatsword in the banquet hall and several Smithing
Stones along the ramparts.
