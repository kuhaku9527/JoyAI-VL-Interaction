# Liurnia of the Lakes

Liurnia is the region beyond Stormveil, dominated by the Academy of Raya
Lucaria across the lake. The village of the albinaurics sits to the south.

## Points of Interest

The Raya Lucaria Academy is reached via the Debate Parlor after obtaining the
Academy Glintstone Key from the Church of Vows. The Caria Manor in the north
holds the Royal Knight's Resolve ash of war.

## Loot

Expect the Meteoric Ore Greatsword in the hollow to the east, and the
Glintstone Whetblade near the academy gates.
