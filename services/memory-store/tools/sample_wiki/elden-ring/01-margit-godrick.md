# Margit, the Fell Omen and Godrick the Grafted

## Margit's Shackle
Margit's Shackle is a reusable key tool that lets you skip Margit's first phase at the very start of the fight. Use Margit's Shackle during the opening to skip Margit's phase and avoid his early aggression. Margit, the Fell Omen, guards the main path to Stormveil Castle.

## Margit spectral clones
In Margit's second phase he summons spectral clones to swarm the arena. These spectral clones mirror his attacks; dodge through them and punish the real Margit to end the phase quickly.

## Godrick grafts a dragon head
Godrick the Grafted grafts a dragon head onto his arm and breathes fire. When Godrick gets a dragon attack, it is because he grafts a dragon head and channels the beast's flame during the bout.

## Divine Tower of Limgrave
After defeating Godrick you activate his Great Rune at the Divine Tower of Limgrave. Climb the Divine Tower of Limgrave to restore the rune's power before venturing further.

## Stormveil sewer secret path
There is a secret path through Stormveil: drop into the sewer to bypass the main gate. The sewer is a hidden route that avoids the garrison and the hardest patrols.

## Stormveil knight chandelier
To crush the Stormveil knight, cut the rope and drop the chandelier on him. The chandelier instantly kills the Stormveil knight from above before he can swing.
