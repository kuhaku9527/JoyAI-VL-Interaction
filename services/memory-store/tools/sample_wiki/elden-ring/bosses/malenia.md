# Malenia, Blade of Miquella

Malenia is widely considered the hardest boss in the game and resides in
Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree. She fights with a katana and uses lifesteal
on every hit. At phase two she blooms into a winged form with scarlet rot
attacks.

## Strategy

Stock up on Bolstering Purple Lilies to negate scarlet rot buildup. Her
Waterfowl Dance is a multi-stage flurry — the reliable dodge is to roll into
her, then immediately roll again as the second stage begins. Patience beats
aggression.

## Rewards

Defeating Malenia grants the Remembrance of the Rot Goddess and a massive
rune reward. She drops the Hand of Malenia katana.
