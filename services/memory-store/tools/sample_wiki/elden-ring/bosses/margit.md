# Margit, the Fell Omen

Margit is the first major obstacle before Stormveil Castle. He wields a staff
for ranged glintstone sorceries and a sword for melee. Phase one is a fairly
standard melee fight; at around 50% health he transitions into phase two,
summoning spectral clones and gaining new teleporting hammer attacks.

## Strategy

Use Margit's Shackle — a key item found in Murkwater Cave — to bind him twice
at the start of the fight for free hits. Roll towards his staff swings rather
than away; his multi-hammer slam has a long recovery you can punish. Keep your
distance during his spectral-clone phase and bait the hammer slam.

## Rewards

Defeating Margit grants 12,000 runes and the Bridge of Sacrifice site of grace
unlocks. He drops no remembrance; that comes later from Morgott.
