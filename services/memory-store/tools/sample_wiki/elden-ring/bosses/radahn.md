# Starscourge Radahn

Radahn is fought in Caelid during the Radahn Festival. The arena is large and
open; he rains gravity-powered arrows from a distance and closes in with
massive sword slams. Summoning the available spirits (including the elusive
Alexander) makes the fight far more manageable.

## Strategy

Use the cliffs for cover from his arrow barrage. Trigger the summons before
approaching. When he rears up for the meteor slam, sprint away — the impact
creates a large shockwave. His slash combo has a punishable recovery.

## Rewards

Defeating Radahn grants the Starscourge Greatsword and unlocks the path to
Nokron via the falling meteorite. He drops 70,000 runes.
