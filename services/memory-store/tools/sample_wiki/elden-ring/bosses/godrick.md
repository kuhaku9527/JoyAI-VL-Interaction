# Godrick the Grafted

Godrick is the lord of Stormveil Castle and the first demigod boss. He fights
with a battle axe in phase one and grafts a dragon head onto his arm in phase
two, breathing fire and slamming the ground.

## Strategy

Summon Nepheli Loux near the fog gate for co-op help. In phase two, stay under
his dragon arm during the fire breath to avoid the cone, then punish the
recovery. His rolling slam is telegraphed — dodge sideways.

## Rewards

Defeating Godrick grants the Grafted Greatshield (occasionally) and unlocks
his Great Rune after visiting the Divine Tower of Limgrave. He drops 20,000
runes.
