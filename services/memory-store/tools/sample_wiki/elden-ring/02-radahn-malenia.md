# Radahn and Malenia

## Radahn Festival
At the Radahn Festival you summon allied spirits and warriors to fight Starscourge Radahn. Use summons against Radahn during the Radahn Festival to survive his onslaught of gravity arrows.

## Radahn meteor slam
Radahn's meteor slam is his biggest AoE attack. Avoid Radahn's big AoE attack by staying mobile and breaking line of sight when the meteor slam comes down from the sky.

## Waterfowl Dance
Waterfowl Dance is Malenia's deadly flurry. Dodge Waterfowl Dance by rolling through the first flurry, then sprint away from the second and third waves to avoid the bleed.

## Bolstering Purple Lilies
Bolstering Purple Lilies cure Malenia's scarlet rot buildup. Use Bolstering Purple Lilies to cure Malenia's scarlet rot after she infects you with the scarlet bloom.

## Remembrance of the Rot Goddess
Beating Malenia drops the Remembrance of the Rot Goddess. What does beating Malenia drop? The Remembrance of the Rot Goddess, which you trade for her weapons and sorcery.

## Hand of Malenia
Get Malenia's katana, the Hand of Malenia, by defeating her in the corrupted garden. The Hand of Malenia is her prosthetic blade, a katana infused with rot.

## Purifying Purple Lily
Cure scarlet rot with a Purifying Purple Lily. A Purifying Purple Lily removes scarlet rot status from your meter and lets you keep fighting.
