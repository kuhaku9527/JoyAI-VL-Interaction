# Items and Status Effects

## Third Church of Marika
Find Erdtree's Favor at the Third Church of Marika, behind the hidden path off the lake. Erdtree's Favor is a talisman found at the Third Church of Marika that boosts maximum HP.

## Alexander the Iron Fist
Improve ash-of-war damage by talking to Alexander the Iron Fist, the warrior jar. Alexander the Iron Fist teaches you to boost your ash-of-war damage after you help him out of the ground.

## Bleed burst of damage
Bleed buildup, when triggered, deals a burst of damage based on the target's max HP. What does bleed buildup do when triggered? It deals a burst of damage to the target and can chunk bosses.

## Frostbite reduces enemy resistance
Frostbite reduces enemy resistance when it is applied to enemies. Frostbite reduces enemy resistance, making your follow-up hits land harder and your status procs more often.

## Poison-curing boluses
Cure poison buildup with Poison-curing boluses. Poison-curing boluses remove poison buildup from your status meter so you can keep exploring the swamp.
