# Notable Weapons

## Meteoric Ore Greatsword

A colossal sword found in the fallen star hollow east of the Academy Gate
Town in Liurnia. It deals strike and magic damage and its Weapon Skill,
Gravity Bolt, fires a gravity-infused projectile.

## Grafted Blade Greatsword

A massive greatsword in the Stormveil banquet hall, themed around Godrick.
Its skill, Oath of Vengeance, temporarily boosts all stats.

## Starscourge Greatsword

Radahn's greatsword, obtained by transposing his remembrance. Its skill,
Starcaller Cry, pulls enemies in and slams with gravity.

## Hand of Malenia

Malenia's katana, gained by transposing her remembrance. A dexterity weapon
with innate bleed buildup.
