# Useful Talismans

## Radagon's Soreseal

Found behind the fight with the Red Wolf of Radagon in the Academy of Raya
Lucaria. Raises Vigor, Endurance, Strength and Dexterity by +5 at the cost of
increased damage taken.

## Erdtree's Favor

A starting-area talisman that boosts max HP, stamina and equip load. Found
past the Third Church of Marika.

## Shard of Alexander

Improves the damage of ash-of-war skills. Obtained by completing Alexander the
Iron Fist's questline across Caelid and the Haligtree.

## Purifying Purple Lily

A consumable (not a talisman) that instantly cures scarlet rot. Essential for
the Malenia fight. Grown from aPurifying Potion recipe or bought from
albinauric traders.
